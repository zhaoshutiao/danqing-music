About Project


This project is designed to help users relax and unwind. Our goal is to naturally combine the beauty of traditional Chinese painting and music, creating a calming and culturally immersive experience. The project is inspired by the famous Chinese painting A Thousand Li of Rivers and Mountains. Through interactive elements, users can explore and engage with the scenery and sounds, experiencing the charm of classical Chinese aesthetics.

There is a moving story behind this masterpiece. Its creator, Wang Ximeng, was a brilliant teenage artist in the late Northern Song Dynasty. At just 18 years old, he completed A Thousand Li of Rivers and Mountains, the only known work he left behind in his short life. Remarkably, with just this one painting, he secured a lasting place in the history of Chinese art.

The painting is in the form of a long handscroll, depicting endless mountains and vast rivers. Throughout the landscape, the artist delicately adds pavilions, bridges, villages, ferries, travelers, fishing scenes, and flying birds, by which capturing a lively and detailed portrayal of life in nature. In its style, the painting blends characteristics of both the southern and northern schools of Chinese landscape art. In terms of color, it follows the blue-and-green painting tradition that dates back to the Tang Dynasty. The use of ochre enhances the vividness of the mineral pigments, especially azurite and malachite, making the scenery more striking and vibrant.

It is both a realistic depiction of the natural world and an idealized expression of beauty and harmony. Today, the painting is housed in the Palace Museum in Beijing and is regarded as a treasure of Chinese art. In recent years, it has once again drawn public attention and become a cultural symbol in the revival of traditional Chinese aesthetics.

About Music


Traditional Chinese music is an essential part of China's rich cultural heritage, with a history spanning thousands of years. Unlike Western music, which often focuses on harmony, Chinese music emphasizes melody and the expression of emotion through individual notes and tones. It is deeply connected to nature, philosophy, and poetry.

In this project, you’ll find examples of iconic instruments such as the bianzhong (ancient bronze bells), dizi (bamboo flute), guzheng (plucked zither), sheng (mouth-blown free reed), guqin (seven-stringed zither), and pipa (pear-shaped lute). Each of these instruments tells its own story and reflects the spirit of Chinese aesthetics

Traditional Chinese music is not only meant to entertain but also to cultivate the spirit and promote harmony between people and the natural world. Whether performed solo or in ensembles, it invites listeners into a tranquil, poetic soundscape that reflects the soul of Chinese culture.
